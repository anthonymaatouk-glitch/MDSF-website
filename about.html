About the Michel Daher Foundation

A local Lebanese non-profit founded in 2014. Our mission is to improve the livelihoods of the Lebanese people, with a particular focus on the Bekaa community, thereby contributing to the Global Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

Our Story

From a spark of kindness to a commitment for change

A journey of empathy, action, and enduring impact.

  1. 1992

    The family decided to return to their village and began charitable work.

  2. 2010

    Planned community healthcare centers, focused on women and children.

  3. 2012

    Started the annual collective wedding for couples from various Christian sects.

  4. 2014

    The foundation was officially registered.

Our Focus

Four pillars of impact

Pillar 1

Community Healthcare

Quality healthcare for all, through our healthcare centers. PCR and COVID-19 vaccination drives reached 12,000+ people in 2021.

Explore Pillar 2

Economic & Human Development

Tech Academy, Career Bridge (6,000+ participants) and Adira, which has empowered 25+ local women by unifying their Mouneh products under one brand.

Explore Pillar 3

Social Development

300+ couples helped through collective weddings, 2,000+ women supported by Ente Adda, and 300+ children with speech and psychomotor therapy.

Explore Pillar 4

Community & Environmental Development

Fakker Bi2iyan: 19 clean-ups, 48 tons recycled, 42 trees planted. After the Beirut Port blast: 200 volunteers and 40 homes repaired.
